Mahmadu Kabba Musa

Former director of the Imam Malik Institute and founder of Karate in The Gambia

Mahmadu Musa Kabba: A Legacy of Discipline, Courage, and Service. Mahmadu Musa Kabba, the former director of the institute, was a pioneering figure in The Gambia. He introduced the art of self‑defense known as Karate to the country and registered it legally, laying the foundation for a national discipline that shaped generations. Under his guidance, countless students trained with dedication including politicians, members of the Gambian Armed Forces, and even the President himself. His impact extended far beyond the dojo, influencing character, discipline, and national pride.
May Allah forgive him and grant him Jannah.

Ebrahim Marry Janaga

Principal

Ebrahim Marry Jagana is a respected Gambian Islamic scholar, educator, preacher, and community leader known for his lifelong dedication to teaching, guidance, and service. Born in 1966 in New Demba kunda, he grew into one of the prominent figures contributing to Islamic education and moral development in The Gambia. He began his Islamic studies in his local community, where he built a strong foundation in Arabic, Islamic jurisprudence, and religious sciences. His pursuit of knowledge later led him to continue his studies at Al‑Azhar University, one of the world’s most recognized centers of Islamic learning, further strengthening his academic and spiritual formation. Throughout his career, Ebrahima Mari Jagana has served as a teacher, preacher, and mentor, shaping generations of students with discipline, clarity, and compassion. His work extends beyond the classroom: he has participated in national Islamic committees, contributed to community development, and supported initiatives aimed at strengthening Islamic values and education across the country. Known for his leadership, excellent command of Arabic, and commitment to service, he continues to play an important role in religious guidance and education in The Gambia. His legacy is marked by dedication, sincerity, and a lifelong mission to uplift his community through knowledge and faith.

Sheikh Shondi Muhammad Samba Drammeh

Vice Principal

Sheikh Shondi Muhammad Samba Drammeh is a distinguished Gambian Islamic preacher, educator, and educational administrator known for his lifelong dedication to teaching, community guidance, and religious service. Born in 1963, he grew up in an environment rooted in traditional Qur’anic learning, beginning his early studies in the well known kuttab circles of his village. In 1975, he transitioned into formal academic studies, marking the beginning of a structured educational journey that later extended beyond The Gambia. He pursued further studies in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, spending two years deepening his knowledge in Islamic sciences and pedagogy. His academic path led him to King Saud University, where he specialized in Islamic Studies Tafsir and Hadith within the College of Education during the years 1992 to 1993. This period shaped his scholarly foundation and prepared him for a career of impactful service. Upon returning to The Gambia, Sheikh Shondi Muhammad Samba Drammeh joined the Imam Malik Islamic Institute, where he has served as a teacher, administrator, and director of general education. His leadership continues to influence the institute’s academic standards and spiritual mission. Beyond the classroom, he is active in national Islamic councils, delivers weekly religious programs on Al-Falah TV and QTV, and provides daily lessons through social media platforms. He also collaborates with Nidaa Charity Center, contributing to community development and religious outreach. Known for his excellent Arabic, strong leadership, and balanced communication skills in English and French, Sheikh Shondi Muhammad Samba Drammeh remains a respected figure in Gambian Islamic education and da‘wah. His legacy is marked by service, scholarship, and a commitment to uplifting society through knowledge and faith.

Malamin Zakaria Jagana

Head of Culture, daa'wah and Guidance

Malamin Zakaria Jagana is a distinguished Gambian educator and Islamic studies teacher whose lifelong dedication to teaching, religious guidance, and community development has made him a respected figure in the field of Islamic education. Born in 1964 in Kindawon, Sierra Leone, he grew up in a family known for its commitment to faith and learning, under the guidance of his parents Alhaji Zakaria Jagana. His educational journey began with memorizing the Qur’an between 1964 and 1971 at the Hajj Yabi Qur’anic School. From 1971 to 1983, he continued his studies in the village of Wounde, strengthening his foundation in Islamic sciences and traditional learning. In 1984 to 1985, he joined the Imam Malik Islamic Institute in Sukuta, where he advanced his knowledge in Arabic, Islamic jurisprudence, and religious studies. Later, in 1996, he pursued further studies in Arabic and French at the Islamic Institute, expanding his linguistic and academic capabilities. His professional journey reflects decades of service and leadership. From 1996 to 2000, he served as the Director of Sudokota School, now known as the Imam Malik Islamic Institute. Between 2000 and 2007, he worked as a teacher, imam, and preacher at the Abdulrahman Mosque. Since 2007, he has been teaching Islamic Education and Arabic Language at the primary, junior, and senior levels, while also supervising da‘wah, guidance, and media activities at the Imam Malik Islamic Institute in Sukuta. He is known for his strong communication skills, his ability to teach across cultures, and his dedication to Islamic education. His work continues to inspire students and strengthen the religious and educational landscape of The Gambia.
