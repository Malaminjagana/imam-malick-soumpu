Best Private School in Kanifing - English, Arabic, Islamic & Quran School in The Gambia

Al-Imam Malick Islamic Institute is among the leading schools in The Gambia, offering English education alongside Arabic and Islamic studies. Located in Kanifing South, Serekunda, we provide private English education and Arabic and Quran memorization programs. Our English curriculum, combined with Arabic and Islamic education, supports students across Kanifing Municipality, Gambia.

A Home of Islamic Learning

Rooted in the teachings of Imam Malik, the institute offers disciplined Islamic education through both its Tahfiz and Madrasa programs, providing strong moral guidance and a learning environment that shapes confident, value‑driven students.

Mahmadu Kabba Musa

Former director of the Imam Malik Institute and founder of Karate in The Gambia

Muhammad Kaba Musa - Rahimahullah was a Gambian educator, former General Director of Imam Malik Islamic Institute, and a pioneering national figure. Born in Kaba Kunda, Basse and a graduate of Al-Azhar University Cairo in 1992, he taught for 13 years at Tallinding Kunja, led Imam Malik Institute, and served as Imam and Khateeb of Sinbo Doqati Mosque. He was also the founder who introduced and legally registered Karate in The Gambia, training generations including politicians, members of the Armed Forces and the President himself. May Allah forgive him and grant him Jannah.
