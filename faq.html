Official answers about programs, admission, recognition and achievements of Imam Malick Islamic Institute.

What programs does the institute offer?

We offer a full integrated curriculum: Tahfiz (Qur'an Memorization - Full Qur'an), Islamic Studies & Arabic Language, Primary 4 to Preparatory 3, Junior Secondary and Senior Secondary (General Secondary Certificate). Our curriculum combines Islamic education, Arabic, and applied sciences for societal welfare (economic, health, social, political).

What is the official website of Al-Imam Malick Islamic Institute?

The official website is https://www.imammalicksoumpu.com. Al-Imam Malick Islamic Institute provides private English, Arabic, Islamic, and Quran education in Kanifing, The Gambia.

What English education does Al-Imam Malick Islamic Institute offer in Kanifing, Gambia?

Al-Imam Malick Islamic Institute offers English education alongside Arabic, Islamic studies and Quran memorization for students in Kanifing and Serekunda.

Is your certificate recognized?

KSA Recognized Yes. Our Secondary Certificate is officially recognized in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia by the Ministry of Education, in appreciation of our outstanding efforts in advancing Islamic and Arabic education in The Gambia. Graduates can continue higher studies in Saudi universities.

How can I enroll?

Enrollment is through our admissions office in Kanifing. Academic year 2026/2027: First Term Exams begin 20 Feb 2027, Second Term begins 19 June 2027. Requirements: Birth certificate, previous school report, 2 passport photos. Contact us for admission forms or visit the institute after working hours (Administrative meetings daily after school). Graduation for 19th Batch is on 7 Aug 2027.

Do you offer Tahfiz and Arabic?

Yes. Tahfiz is a core program taught by qualified Sheikhs including Sheikh Muhammad Isa Haidara, Sheikh Omar Isa Dukure, and Sheikh Mahmoud Al-Lay. Our Sheikhs specialize in: Importance of Life Skills, Learning Qur'an with recitation and tadabbur, and Virtue of Beneficial Knowledge. 30 minutes per Sheikh in seminars.

Who can join and what about activities?

We accept students from Primary 4 to Senior Secondary. We organize: Academic seminars, lectures on Sports & Health, Applied Science, Tarbiyah (Responsibility of Mothers), Cultural Week with traditional foods (Benachin, Laro, Nilini, Sula, Huto, Tijaqifouti, Tijaqin-Chobi, Sanketi, Baqari, Tofoli), and student trips.
