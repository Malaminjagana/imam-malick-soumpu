Teacher • Preacher • Orator • Al-Azhar Graduate

Muhammad Muhammad Touray

A Gambian educator, preacher and orator dedicated to teaching, guidance and community service. A graduate of Al-Azhar Al-Sharif, serving as teacher and Head of the English Department at Imam Malik Institute and as Imam and Khateeb of Abu al-Khair Mosque, Sukuta.

Born 10 Oct 1973 Koinya Kantora, The Gambia Al-Azhar University Imam & Teacher

From the Qur'anic learning circles of Gamibisara to the lecture halls of Al-Azhar in Cairo, the path of Muhammad Muhammad Touray has been shaped by a lifelong commitment to learning the religion and teaching it in the classroom, on the minbar, and through the airwaves.

His Original Biography

Beginnings

Where the Journey Started

Born on 10 October 1973 in Koinya Kantora, The Gambia, Muhammad Muhammad Touray grew up in a family rooted in faith son of Muhammad Touray and Hajja Fatima Mama Omani. His early education began in the traditional majlis of the late Al-Haj Babou Fouti in Gamibisara, where he built his foundation in Qur'an and Islamic sciences.

That early grounding sparked a desire to seek knowledge beyond his village, taking him to renowned centers of learning across West Africa and to Egypt.

Educational Journey

Seeking Knowledge Across Three Countries

His pursuit of knowledge reflects the classic path of West African scholars moving between local scholars, specialized institutes, and university education.

Early Years

Traditional Studies Gamibisara

Began his studies in the council of the late Al-Haj Babou Fouti in the village of Gamibisara, learning Qur'an and foundational Islamic sciences.

1993

Dar al-Qur'an and Hadith Tuba, Mali

Traveled to Tuba, Mali to study at Dar al-Qur'an and Hadith, deepening his knowledge in Qur'anic and Hadith sciences.

1997

Institute of Islamic Research Cairo, Egypt

Joined the Islamic Research Institute (Ma'had al-Buhuth al-Islamiyya) in Cairo, affiliated with Al-Azhar, where he completed his secondary education with a very good grade.

2002

Al-Azhar University Faculty of Education, English Department

Admitted to the prestigious Al-Azhar University in Cairo, Faculty of Education, English Department. He obtained his Licence (Bachelor's degree) with a good grade, combining Islamic education with strong English language training.

Qualifications

Academic Milestones

His academic record shows consistent excellence: a primary certificate with excellent honors from Dar al-Qur'an and Hadith, a secondary certificate with very good honors from the Al-Azhar Islamic Research Institute, and a university licence from Al-Azhar University's Faculty of Education.

This combination of traditional grounding and modern university training gave him the ability to teach in both Arabic and English, and to connect with diverse audiences.

Languages & Strengths

A Voice That Connects

Muhammad Muhammad Touray is known for his strong communication skills and ability to reach people across linguistic communities in The Gambia.

His mother tongue is Soninke, alongside fluency in Mandinka and Wolof. In Arabic, he has very good command in both speaking and writing the language of his advanced studies. In English, the official language of The Gambia, he is also highly proficient in speaking and writing, a result of his specialization at Al-Azhar.

This multilingual ability, combined with years of experience in teaching, administration, da'wah, and khutbah, has made him a trusted teacher, orator, and guide in his community.
