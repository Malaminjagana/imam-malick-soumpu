Muhammad Kaowjed Camara

A Gambian teacher of Arabic language and Islamic studies with long experience in teaching and administration. From the Kuttab of Drami family to a degree in Arabic and Islamic Studies, he has taught at Imam Malik Institute since 2007 and continues his mission in education and student development.

Dimanaka Qadima, Yassi The Gambia Teaching Since 2007 Director of Examinations

From learning the basics of reading in the Kuttab of the Drami family to leading examinations and student affairs at Imam Malik Institute, Muhammad Kaowjed Camara has dedicated his life to teaching, nurturing student confidence, and organizing educational life with discipline and care.

His Original Biography

Early Learning

Where It All Began

Born in Dimanaka Qadima, Yassi District, Republic of The Gambia, Muhammad Kaowjed Camara began his educational journey before 1991 in the Kuttab of Al Drami in Baghrini, under the tutelage of Sheikh Al-Hajj Bakr Drami may Allah preserve him where he learned the principles of reading and foundational Islamic knowledge.

This traditional beginning instilled in him a love for learning and teaching that would shape his entire career.

Educational Journey

A Path From Kuttab to University

Before 1991

Kuttab Drami Family, Baghrini

Started his learning in the Kuttab of Al Drami, under Sheikh Al-Hajj Bakr Drami, where he learned the basics of reading and Qur'anic principles.

1991 - 2000

Salafi Association School Sheikh Mahmoud Sissi Camara Village

Joined the school of the Salafi Association in 1991 in the village of Sheikh Mahmoud Sissi Camara — may Allah have mercy on him. Studied Fiqh and all Islamic sciences, completing his primary stage in 2000.

2000 - 2007

Islamic Union Institute Serekunda (Landil King)

Moved to Serekunda and enrolled at the Islamic Union Institute (Landil King). Studied the preparatory and secondary stages from 2000 to 2007, successfully completing his secondary education.

2011 - 2015

College of Islamic Da'wah Senegal Branch & Arabic Studies

In 2011, he gained admission to the College of Islamic Da'wah — Senegal Branch. Graduated in 2015 with a Bachelor's degree (Licence) in Arabic Language and Islamic Studies.

Professional Experience

Teaching and Returning to Serve

His teaching career began immediately after his secondary graduation and has continued without interruption.

2007 - 2011

Teacher Imam Malik Islamic Institute

Appointed as a teacher at Imam Malik Islamic Institute after graduating from the secondary stage, serving from 2007 to 2011.

2015 - Present

Teacher Imam Malik Islamic Institute

After graduating from university, he returned to The Gambia and resumed teaching at Imam Malik Islamic Institute since 2015, continuing his message in education and serving students of knowledge.

Philosophy

Education With Purpose

His approach combines classroom teaching with student development and administrative precision.
