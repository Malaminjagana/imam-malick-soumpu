A Gambian da'iyah and teacher of Shari'ah sciences, striving to spread knowledge and da'wah to Allah with wisdom and beautiful preaching, and to build a generation rooted in knowledge, faith, and service to the community.

His Original Biography

Background

A Life Dedicated to Knowledge and Da'wah

Born in 1969 in The Gambia, Jafara Foday Gumaneh began seeking Islamic knowledge at an early age. From 1988 onward, he devoted himself to learning, memorizing, and studying under scholars, before moving into teaching and da'wah.

His vision is clear and consistent: to spread authentic Shari'ah knowledge, invite to Allah with wisdom and good advice, and contribute to building a faithful, educated generation that serves its religion and community through learning, guidance, and good character.

Education

Academic Formation

His educational path was built within The Gambia's leading Islamic institutes, combining Shari'ah studies with training in education.

Shari'ah Secondary

Institute of Islamic Sciences The Gambia

Completed his Shari'ah secondary certificate at the Institute of Islamic Sciences in The Gambia, establishing his foundation in Islamic sciences.

Higher Diploma

Imam Malik Islamic Institute The Gambia

Obtained a higher diploma in Islamic studies from Imam Malik Islamic Institute, deepening his studies in Qur'an, Hadith, Fiqh and Seerah.

Specialization

Diploma in Shari'ah and Education

Completed a specialized diploma combining Shari'ah and Islamic education methodology, preparing him for teaching and curriculum development.
