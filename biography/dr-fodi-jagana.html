His Original Biography

Early Life & Education

From Dakar's Classrooms to Madinah's Faculty of Sharia

Dr. Fodi Jagana's academic journey began in Dakar, Senegal, where he completed his primary, middle, and secondary education at schools of the Al-Falah Movement — a foundation that set him on a path of formal, structured learning from an early age.

That foundation carried him to Saudi Arabia, where he joined the Faculty of Sharia at the Islamic University of Madinah. It was there that his academic path deepened significantly, moving step by step from undergraduate to doctoral study.

Scholarly Output

Skills & Expertise

Research, Teaching & Institutional Leadership

Dr. Fodi Jagana's expertise spans scientific research and verification, university-level teaching, writing and authorship, preaching and guidance, and the management of educational programmes and institutions.

This range of skills reflects a career built as much on the page as on the pulpit — someone equally at home producing academic research as he is teaching a lecture hall or delivering a Friday sermon.

Legacy

A Scholar's Path, From Dakar to Madinah and Home

The path of Dr. Fodi Jagana traces a clear arc: from early schooling in Dakar, through a bachelor's, master's, and doctorate at the Islamic University of Madinah, to a career of teaching, research, and religious leadership back home in The Gambia.

His published research on fiqh, moderation, and the lives of the prophets — alongside his book on local Islamic history — reflects a scholar committed not only to studying knowledge, but to recording and sharing it for future generations.

Today, through his work at two universities, an Islamic institute, and a mosque, he continues to carry that same mission forward: knowledge pursued, lived, and passed on.
