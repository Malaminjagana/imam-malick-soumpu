Educator • Qur'an Memorization Specialist • Da'wah Supervisor

Omar Issa Dukureh

An educator and specialist in Qur'an memorization and Islamic education. From traditional learning in Sierra Leone to leading educational roles at Imam Malik Institute in The Gambia since 2001, his life has been dedicated to teaching the Qur'an and serving da'wah.

Born 1972 Sierra Leone Gambian Nationality Imam Malik Institute

The journey of Omar Issa Dukureh bridges two West African nations and over three decades of service to the Qur'an. From memorizing the Book of Allah to supervising its memorization for generations of students, his path is one of dedication to education and da'wah.

His Original Biography

Background

From Sierra Leone to The Gambia

Omar Issa Dukureh was born in 1972 in the Republic of Sierra Leone, West Africa, and holds Gambian nationality. Belonging to the Dukureh family, he received his early traditional education in Sierra Leone before his educational journey brought him to The Gambia, where he has since built his life of service.

His formation combines traditional West African Islamic learning with formal Islamic education, centered on the Qur'an and its sciences.

Educational Journey

Learning, Memorizing, and Teaching the Qur'an

His educational path reflects a lifelong relationship with the Holy Qur'an — as a student, memorizer, and later as a teacher and supervisor.

Early Years

Traditional Education — Sierra Leone

Received his traditional Islamic education in Sierra Leone, building a foundation in Qur'an and basic Islamic sciences.

Foundation

Dar al-Qur'an and Hadith Primary & Preparatory

Completed his primary and preparatory (intermediate) studies at Dar al-Qur'an and Hadith, focusing on Qur'an memorization and Hadith sciences.

1984

Qur'an Memorization School Al-Khair Institute

In 1984, he joined the Qur'an memorization department. He devoted himself to memorizing the Holy Qur'an, completing his memorization and receiving recognition for his efforts.

1988

Completion of Qur'an Memorization

Completed the memorization of the entire Qur'an in 1988, marking a major milestone that would define his future service in Qur'an education.

1992 to 2001

Academic Qualifications The Gambia

Obtained his intermediate and secondary qualifications in The Gambia between 1992 and 1996, followed by higher studies. In 2001, he completed advanced studies in Shari'ah and Islamic education, with focus on Qur'an sciences and teaching methodology.

Professional Journey

Leading Qur'an Education at Imam Malik Institute

Since 2001, Omar Issa Dukureh has held a series of key educational, administrative and da'wah responsibilities, particularly at Imam Malik Institute.

2001 to2002

First Director Imam Malik Islamic Institute, Serekunda

Served as the first Director of Imam Malik Islamic Institute in Serekunda, establishing its early educational programs and administrative structure.

2003

Deputy Director

Served as Deputy Director of the institute, supporting academic planning and daily administration.

2003 to Present

Supervisor of Qur'an Memorization Department

Supervisor of the Qur'an memorization department, overseeing halaqas, memorization programs, and student progress.

Leadership

Deputy Director of Educational Affairs

Deputy Director for Educational Affairs, responsible for academic quality, curriculum implementation, and teacher coordination.

Da'wah

Supervisor of Da'wah Program — Sheikh Hassan Bin Muhammad Al Sheikh

Supervisor of the da'wah program affiliated with Sheikh Hassan Bin Muhammad Al Sheikh in The Gambia, coordinating Islamic awareness and outreach activities.

Languages & Character

A Bridge Between Communities

Fluent in multiple languages reflecting his cross-cultural journey: Arabic, English, Soninke, Mandinka, Malinke, and Krio (Sierra Leonean). This linguistic diversity enables him to connect with students and communities across The Gambia and Sierra Leone.

His work is marked by dedication to the Qur'an, commitment to education, and a focus on building strong memorization programs that preserve the Book of Allah for future generations.
