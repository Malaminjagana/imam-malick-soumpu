His Original Biography

Personal Background

Who is Dr. Kisma Sheikh Sahoo?

Dr. Kisma Sheikh Sahoo is a Gambian scholar of Islamic sciences, a preacher (da'iyah), researcher, and teacher. He originates from Basse area, The Gambia, and holds Gambian nationality.

His profile describes him as an academic with wide scientific and administrative experience in teaching, research, da'wah, and institutional work both in The Gambia and in Saudi Arabia with a clear passion for spreading Islamic knowledge and serving society.

Work Experience

Teaching, Preaching & Institutional Service

Since 1998, Dr. Sahoo has been active as an imam, preacher, and teacher. His experience includes years of teaching in Islamic schools, preaching in mosques, and serving in educational administration.

His record includes extensive work in da'wah and teaching in The Gambia, supervising research, mentoring students, and participating in Islamic committees. He has also served in roles connected to the Hajj mission (2001-2006 experience as guide/educator for pilgrims) and worked with Islamic institutes for more than 20 years.

Between his studies in Madinah and his work in The Gambia, he has built a profile that combines academic research with practical community education.

Service Beyond the Classroom

Dr. Sahoo is a member of several Islamic bodies. His CV notes membership in the Higher Islamic Council of The Gambia and other scientific and da'wah associations, including roles related to Fatwa and Islamic guidance.
