Muhammad Jola Camara

A Gambian teacher of the Holy Qur'an and its sciences. From memorizing the Qur'an in Demba Kunda in 2001 to earning a Master's in Sudan, he now teaches Tafsir and Tajweed at Imam Malik Institute and contributes to its educational and financial administration.

Born 10 Feb 1987 Demba Kunda, The Gambia Master's Degree 2014/2015 Qur'an Teacher Since 2001

The story of Muhammad Jola Camara begins in Demba Kunda and extends to the universities of Sudan a journey driven by a single purpose: to serve the Book of Allah through learning, teaching, and building faithful Qur'anic generations.

His Original Biography

Beginnings

From Demba Kunda to the Qur'an

Born on 10 February 1987 in Demba Kunda, The Gambia, Muhammad Jola Camara began his educational journey in his village at the school of the Salafi Islamic Association. His early inclination toward the Qur'an led him to the Sheikh al-Islam Ibn Taymiyyah Qur'anic School in Bantan, where he studied under Sheikh Ahmad Somali.

In 2001, he completed the memorization of the Holy Qur'an a foundational achievement that shaped his entire future path as a teacher and educator.

Academic Journey

An Academic Path to Sudan

After completing his Hifz, he joined the Islamic Institute in Banjul, completing his preparatory and secondary studies in the academic year 2006/2007. Seeking higher Islamic education, he traveled to Sudan.

2001

Hifz al-Qur'an Sheikh al-Islam Ibn Taymiyyah School

Certificate of memorization of the Holy Qur'an, with proficiency and Tajweed, under the supervision of Sheikh Ahmad Somali.

2006 / 2007

Secondary Certificate Islamic Institute

Completed secondary studies at the Islamic Institute, building the academic foundation for university education.

2010 / 2011

Bachelor's Degree International University of Africa, Sudan

Traveled to Sudan and enrolled at the International University of Africa, Faculty of the Holy Qur'an. Obtained his Bachelor's degree in Qur'anic studies.

2014 / 2015

Master's Degree University of the Holy Qur'an and Islamic Sciences, Sudan

Continued his postgraduate studies at the University of the Holy Qur'an and Islamic Sciences in Sudan, earning his Master's degree in Qur'anic and Islamic sciences.
