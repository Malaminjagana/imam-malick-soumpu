Muhammad Kaba Musa

رحمه الله - May Allah have mercy on him

A Gambian educator and leader in Islamic education who dedicated over 30 years to teaching, da'wah, and educational administration. From learning at the hands of his father Al-Hajj Musa Kaba to graduating from Al-Azhar University Cairo, his legacy continues through the students and institutions he served.

Kaba Kunda Basse The Gambia Al-Azhar 1992 Former General Director

From Kaba Kunda in Basse to the College of Education at Al-Azhar University in Cairo, the late Muhammad Kaba Musa رحمه الله embodied the importance of teaching and nurturing for the community a mission he carried from Tallinding Kunja to the leadership of Imam Malik Islamic Institute until his passing.

His Original Biography

Beginnings

Rooted in Family and Knowledge

Born into the Kaba Kunda family in Basi Ongari, Upper River Region, The Gambia, he began his education under the supervision of his father Al-Hajj Musa Kaba, then under his elder brother Al-Hajj Muhammad Baghori Kaba a traditional foundation that instilled in him a love for Islamic knowledge.

Seeking higher learning, he traveled to Cairo, Arab Republic of Egypt, where he pursued formal studies in Arabic and Islamic education.

Academic Journey

From Cairo to Service

Preparatory & Secondary

Cairo, Egypt

Completed his preparatory and secondary certificates in Cairo, building a strong academic foundation in Arabic and Islamic sciences.

1992

Licence - College of Education, Al-Azhar University Cairo

Obtained his university degree (Licence) from the College of Education at the prestigious Al-Azhar University in Cairo. Graduated in 1992 with Higher Licence in Education.

Professional Journey

Three Decades of Educational Leadership

1992 - 2003

Teacher Islamic Institute, Tallinding Kunja

Returned to The Gambia immediately after graduation and began working at the Islamic Institute in Tallinding Kunja. He served for 13 years as a teacher, then was appointed Deputy Director of Examinations, Deputy Director of Culture, Da'wah and Guidance, and Director of Activities until 2003-2004.

2004 - Until his passing

Former Teacher & General Director - Imam Malik Islamic Institute

Transferred to Imam Malik Islamic Institute affiliated with Sinbo Doqati Association. He served as teacher, then as General Director of Imam Malik Institute and its branches. He also served as Imam and Khateeb at Sinbo Doqati Mosque, with regular halaqas in multiple mosques and lectures in rural villages throughout The Gambia.

Mission

Education and Its Importance for Society

His core conviction was that teaching and nurturing are among the most important activities for society. This philosophy drove his work both inside Imam Malik Institute and beyond in mosques, villages, and community gatherings. His students and colleagues continue to carry this legacy forward.
