Muhammad Kaba Musa

Mahmadu Musa Kabba - رحمه الله

Gambian educator and General Director of Imam Malik Islamic Institute. Born in Kaba Kunda, Basse, and a graduate of Al-Azhar University Cairo in 1992 with a Higher Licence in Education, he taught for 13 years at the Islamic Institute Tallinding Kunja, then served as Deputy Director of Examinations and Director of Activities, before leading Imam Malik Institute and serving as Imam and Khateeb of Sinbo Doqati Mosque with lectures across villages and mosques.

Kaba Kunda - Basse Al-Azhar 1992 Former Director - Imam Malik Pioneer of Karate in The Gambia

From Kaba Kunda in Basse to Al-Azhar in Cairo, and from the classrooms of Tallinding Kunja to founding a national discipline the life of Mahmadu Musa Kabba was a legacy of knowledge, discipline, courage and service.

Academic Journey

From Family Kuttab to Al-Azhar

Born in Kaba Kunda family, Basi Ongari, Upper River Region. He began learning from his father Al-Hajj Musa Kaba and his elder brother Al-Hajj Muhammad Baghori Kaba, before traveling to Cairo where he earned his preparatory, secondary and Licence from the College of Education, Al-Azhar University, graduating in 1992.

1992 - 2003

Islamic Institute, Tallinding Kunja - 13 Years

Teacher for 13 years, then Deputy Director of Examinations, Deputy Director of Culture, Da'wah and Guidance, and Director of Activities until 2003-2004.

2004 - Until Passing

Imam Malik Islamic Institute - Former General Director

Teacher, then General Director of Imam Malik Institute and its branches. Imam and Khateeb of Sinbo Doqati Mosque, with halaqas in many mosques and lectures in villages and rural areas.

A National Pioneer

Mahmadu Musa Kabba: A Legacy of Discipline, Courage, and Service

Mahmadu Musa Kabba, the former director of the institute, was a pioneering figure in The Gambia.

The Founder of Karate in The Gambia

He introduced the art of self-defense known as Karate to the country and registered it legally, laying the foundation for a national discipline that shaped generations. Under his guidance, countless students trained with dedication including politicians, members of the Gambian Armed Forces, and even the President himself. His impact extended far beyond the dojo, influencing character, discipline, and national pride.

May Allah forgive him and grant him Jannah.
