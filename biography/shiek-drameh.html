A Life of Knowledge, Teaching & Da'wah

Shondi Muhammad Samba Dramme

A Gambian Islamic scholar, preacher, teacher, and educational administrator whose journey has been shaped by learning, teaching, religious guidance, and community service.

Born 1963 The Gambia Islamic Scholar Teacher & Preacher

From the traditional circles of Islamic learning to advanced studies in Saudi Arabia and at King Saud University, Shondi Muhammad Samba Dramme has spent his life pursuing knowledge and turning that knowledge into teaching, preaching, and service to his community.

His Original Biography

Early Life

Where His Journey Began

Shondi Muhammad Samba Dramme was born on 13 August 1963 in The Gambia. From an early stage of his life, he developed a strong connection with Islamic learning and the traditional scholarly environment of his community.

His early pursuit of knowledge began in established Islamic learning circles known for their books and scholarship. This traditional foundation became the starting point for a much longer educational journey.

The Pursuit of Knowledge

From Traditional Learning to Higher Education

Education remained at the heart of Shondi Muhammad Samba Dramme's journey. Over the years, his studies progressed from traditional Islamic learning to formal education and specialised studies in Islamic disciplines.

Early Studies

Traditional Islamic Learning

He began seeking knowledge in scholarly learning circles known for their books and religious sciences.

1975

Beginning of Formal Education

In 1975, he began his formal, structured education, continuing to develop both his academic and religious knowledge.

1985

Studies in Saudi Arabia

In 1985, he received a scholarship to study in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia at the Ma'had al-Haram al-Makki. He remained there for two years, deepening his knowledge and educational formation.

1992–1993

King Saud University

Following his studies in Saudi Arabia, he was admitted to King Saud University, Faculty of Education, where he specialised in Islamic Studies, with a focus on Tafsir and Hadith.

Teaching & Education

Turning Knowledge Into Teaching

After returning from his educational journey, Shondi Muhammad Samba Dramme began putting his knowledge into practice through teaching and educational administration.

He began teaching at the Imam Malik Islamic Institute, an institution associated with the Islamic community organisation identified in his profile. Alongside teaching, he took on administrative responsibilities and eventually served as the institute's general inspector.

His role as both teacher and educational administrator reflects a commitment not only to transmitting knowledge, but also to supporting the institutions through which Islamic education is delivered.

Da'wah & Religious Guidance

Sharing Knowledge With the Wider Community

His work extends beyond formal education. Shondi Muhammad Samba Dramme has remained active in religious guidance and da'wah, using mosques, community groups, media, and other platforms to share Islamic knowledge.

He serves as an imam and preacher at the mosque of the Imam Malik Islamic Institute, where he also delivers Friday sermons and contributes to the religious life of the community.

His profile also records regular religious lessons delivered through social communication groups, including WhatsApp, allowing his teaching to reach people beyond the physical classroom and mosque.

Community & Religious Activities

Service Beyond the Classroom

Shondi Muhammad Samba Dramme's work is closely connected to the wider Muslim community. His profile identifies him as a member of several Islamic agencies and councils in The Gambia.

He also collaborates with a charitable medical centre in the field of da'wah and education, reflecting an approach to community service that brings religious guidance together with wider social benefit.

Through his mosque activities, teaching, preaching, community lessons, and organisational involvement, he continues to contribute to Islamic education and religious life in The Gambia.

Religious Media

Taking Religious Education to the Airwaves

His educational work also extends into television. According to his profile, he presents weekly religious programmes on television, including broadcasts through Falaj TV and Q TV, with programmes aired on Thursdays and Fridays.

This use of media allows his religious teaching and guidance to reach audiences beyond the traditional classroom and mosque.

Skills & Communication

Language, Leadership & Teaching

His profile highlights strong Arabic-language ability, alongside intermediate English and French skills.

He is also described as having very strong leadership abilities, complementing his long-standing work as a teacher, preacher, imam, and educational administrator.

Legacy

The journey of Shondi Muhammad Samba Dramme reflects the connection between seeking knowledge and serving others. His work demonstrates a commitment to making Islamic knowledge accessible through different channels: education, preaching, the mosque, community lessons, Islamic organisations, and television.

Through these efforts, he continues to contribute to the religious and educational life of his community, carrying forward a lifelong mission of knowledge, guidance, and service.
