Professor of Hadith • Author • Researcher • Verifier

Dr. Ali Jagana

Professor of Noble Hadith and Islamic Studies. A researcher and author whose academic path led from The Gambia to the Islamic University of Madinah, where he completed his Bachelor's, Master's and Doctorate in Hadith and Islamic Studies.

PhD in Hadith Sciences Islamic University of Madinah Sukuta, The Gambia Author of 4 Books

The academic journey of Dr. Ali Jagana is rooted in a deep commitment to the Noble Hadith. From joining the Islamic University of Madinah in 2004 to becoming a professor, author, and verifier, his work focuses on preserving, explaining, and investigating the Sunnah.

His Original Biography

Academic Formation

From The Gambia to Madinah

Dr. Ali Jagana joined the Islamic University of Madinah in 2004, undertaking the full academic track from undergraduate to postgraduate studies in the Faculty of Hadith and Islamic Studies. He completed this demanding journey in 2019.

His specialization has always been centered on the sciences of the prophetic tradition its narration, explanation, authentication, and preservation.

2004

Enrollment at the Islamic University of Madinah

Joined the Faculty of Hadith and Islamic Studies to begin his higher Islamic education in the City of the Prophet.

2012

Master's Degree Hadith Sciences

Thesis titled: Al-Izhar Sharh al-Masabih from the Chapter of Intent and Excess to the Chapter of Shaving and Haircutting. A critical study and verification of a classical Hadith commentary manuscript.

2019

Doctorate in Hadith and Islamic Studies

Doctoral dissertation focusing on classical Hadith literature, textual verification, and scholarly annotation, continuing his work on manuscript investigation and commentary.

2024

Second Doctorate Arabic Language

Completed a second doctoral thesis in Arabic linguistics, titled Al-Kalimat al-Daqiqa wa Rana Wasfiha a study on precise linguistic expressions, presented through the Islamic University of Madinah's program in Senegal.

Authored Works

Books and Scholarly Contributions

Dr. Ali Jagana is the author of several printed works that combine poetic composition, biographical scholarship, and Hadith literature.

1. Sharh al-Arjuza al-Mi'iyya fi Dhikr Hal Ashraf al-Bariyya A commentary on the famous 100-line poem on the Seerah of the Prophet, peace be upon him. (Printed)
2. Al-Qawl al-'Ilmi fi Sharh Zad al-Talib A scholarly explanation in the field of Hadith studies. (Printed)
3. Al-Diwan al-Shi'ri A collection of his own poetry reflecting Islamic themes and literary expression. (Printed)
4. Al-Jami' al-Nafis lil Mansubat A comprehensive compilation of attributed narrations and texts. (Printed)

Approach

Researcher, Teacher, and Verifier

Dr. Ali Jagana combines the three core roles of the classical Muhaddith: searching for knowledge, verifying it, and teaching it. His work reflects academic precision, literary depth, and a strong commitment to authentic transmission.
