His Original Biography

Early Life & Education

A Long Road Through the Sciences of the Qur'an

Born on 25 May 1987, Dr. Suleiman Muhammad Camara began his education in a village Kuttab, under the guidance of Sheikh Haj Fodi Hekru Kiya — may God have mercy on him. From there, his education advanced steadily through a series of Islamic institutions, each stage building toward a deep specialisation in the Qur'anic sciences.

1996

Primary Education

Graduated from the primary stage at the Islamic School of Jama'a al-Salafiyya in Dembakunda.

2000

Middle School Certificate

Earned his middle school certificate at the Islamic Institute in Talinding, Kanifing.

2001

Qur'an Memorisation Certificate

Completed his certificate in the memorisation of the Holy Qur'an at Al-Noor Islamic Institute.

2003–2007

Dual Secondary Education

Completed secondary studies at the Islamic Institute in Talinding, Kanifing, then pursued a second course of secondary study at a religious institute in Qatar, completed in 2007.

2007–2013

Bachelor's Degree, Madinah

Admitted in 2007 to the Faculty of the Noble Qur'an at the Islamic University of Madinah, earning his bachelor's degree in 2013.

2017

Master's Degree & Ijazah in the Seven Qira'at

Completed his master's degree, and in the same year obtained an Ijazah in the Seven Qira'at through study circles at the Prophet's Mosque in Madinah.

2022

Doctorate

Completed his doctoral studies, earning the title of Dr. Suleiman Muhammad Camara.

Language & Communication

A Voice Across Four Languages

Dr. Camara's linguistic ability mirrors the breadth of communities he has served. Soninke is his mother tongue, and he holds excellent command of Arabic and Mandinka, alongside very good proficiency in English — a range that allows his teaching and preaching to reach a wide cross-section of Gambian society.

Legacy

A Journey Rooted in the Qur'an

The path of Dr. Suleiman Muhammad Camara traces one continuous thread from beginning to end: a lifelong closeness to the Qur'an. From memorisation as a child in a village Kuttab, to years of teaching in the study circles of the Prophet's Mosque, to earning an Ijazah in the Seven Qira'at and a doctorate, his story is built on discipline, recitation, and scholarship.

Today, as a university lecturer, examinations director, and dean, he carries that same discipline into institutional leadership shaping not only students, but the structures through which Islamic education is delivered in The Gambia.

As an imam, khatib, and certified reciter, his voice continues to guide congregations just as his scholarship continues to guide students.
