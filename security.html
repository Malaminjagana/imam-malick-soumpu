Information Security Policy

Al-Imam Malick Islamic Institute - Under Sumpundoukati Society, Kanifing, The Gambia
Knowledge is Light, Action is Worship - العلم نور والعمل عبادة

Al-Imam Malick Islamic Institute (AMII) under Sumpundoukati Society, Kanifing, The Gambia, is committed to protecting the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of all information collected, stored, or processed through our official website, student portal, and internal systems. This policy applies to students, parents, staff, and visitors of our Islamic and Arabic educational programs.

Scope: This policy covers our website, student records, Tahfiz & examination data, administrative meetings, and award certificates recognized by the Ministry of Education of KSA and the General Secretariat for Islamic and Arabic Education.

1 Data Protection & Confidentiality

We take appropriate technical and organizational measures to safeguard personal and institutional data from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. Student personal data (admission forms, birth certificates, exam results for Junior/Senior Secondary) is classified as confidential.

  • Encryption of admission and graduation records (19th Batch 2027 included)
  • No sharing of student Tahfiz progress without parental consent

2 Secure Website & Infrastructure

Our official website uses modern security standards:

  • Encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS 1.3), secure hosting with regular updates
  • Protection of Events & News 2026/2027, Awards (/awards/*.webp) and student trip galleries
  • Daily backups and firewall protection

3 User Privacy & Consent

Personal information submitted through admission forms, contact forms, or during administrative meetings after working hours is handled with care and used only for legitimate educational purposes: enrollment, examinations (First Term 20 Feb 2027, Second Term 19 June 2027), and communication about cultural week and student trips to Banjul, Kachikally, Abuko, and Fathala Zoo Senegal.

4 Access Control & Staff Responsibility

Only authorized staff have access to sensitive information. Access is granted based on role:

  • Academic Committee (Sheikhs: Muhammad Isa Haidara, Omar Isa Dukure, Mahmoud Al-Lay, Muhammad Toure)
  • Administration - full access to enrollment and financial records
  • Teachers - limited to their class sections (Primary 4 to Preparatory 3)

5 Data Storage & Retention

Information is stored securely on encrypted servers and retained only as long as necessary for administrative, educational, or legal purposes. Graduation certificates, WAMY membership documents, and KSA recognition letters are archived permanently with restricted access.

6 Third-Party Services & WAMY

When using external services (hosting, analytics, or World Assembly of Muslim Youth - WAMY platform), we ensure they follow industry-standard security practices and do not sell student data. We are an official member of WAMY.

7 Incident Response & National Compliance

In the event of a security breach or suspicious activity, we take immediate action to investigate, contain, and resolve the issue in coordination with the General Secretariat for Islamic and Arabic Education in The Gambia. Incidents affecting our Best Performing Institute award data or Quran Competition results are reported within 24 hours.

8 Policy Updates & Contact

This Information Security Policy may be updated periodically to reflect improvements, new regulations, or changes in technology. For questions about this policy, contact the institute administration in Kanifing after working hours. Students are encouraged to review this policy during Cultural Week and administrative sessions.

Our Commitment: As the Best Performing Islamic Institute in The Gambia and 1st Place holder in National Quran Competition (Full Quran twice), we uphold the highest standards of Amanah (trust) in protecting your information, following Islamic principles of privacy and trustworthiness.
